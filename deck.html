Problem · for tech founders who just raised

Startups do qualifying R&D every sprint. Few turn it into cash.

34,414C-corps claimed the federal credit in 2022, up 75% from 2015

Up to $500Ka year against payroll tax for companies under $5M revenue, even with no profit

Why now: the payroll election only counts on a return filed on time; the offset is limited to your first 5 revenue years; Form 6765 Section G requires project detail from tax year 2026; and missed years expire after about 3 years.

Solution

Helps a newly funded founder get cash back, with a claim that holds up

1 · Estimate→2 · Prove it from GitHub→3 · File (7-step guide)→4 · CPA packet or partner match

Estimate

Federal credit, payroll-tax offset (split across Social Security and Medicare), state credits. Separates cash you can get now from credits banked for future tax bills, and shows it as runway.

Timeline

When the cash really arrives: the quarter after you file, through Form 8974, and how many quarters it takes at your payroll.

Audit trail

A project log built around the IRS four-part test and the 2026 Form 6765 Section G fields. Markdown export for your CPA.

GitHub evidence

Reads public commit history (with consent), flags R&D signals, suggests projects and R&D share per person, and adds dated, linked evidence to the audit trail.

File & claim

Seven steps from eligibility to Form 6765, the payroll election deadline and quarterly Form 8974. Ends in a CPA packet or a consent-based partner intro: the lead-gen outcome.

Technical implementation

No backend, so no user data to leak

Stack

Static site on GitHub Pages. Vanilla JS; D3 + topojson load only when the map opens. Public-data snapshots ship in data.js.

Live data

USAspending and Grants.gov are called from the browser with a 6-second timeout and fall back to dated snapshots.

Estimator model

Alternative simplified credit (6% or 14% above half the base), optional 280C election, $250K Social Security + $250K Medicare offset caps, 2026 wage base, state ASC and refundability rules.

Privacy by design

Inputs never leave the browser. The audit log lives in localStorage, exports only on click, and "Delete my data" wipes it. No accounts, cookies or analytics.

Roughly $1.5M–$20M of research spend and $90K–$1.2M of credit a year. Under $5M revenue, so the payroll offset applies. Too small for Big 4 firms. Distribution through VCs, accelerators, payroll providers and startup banks.
